# SOXL early-session reversal research

User requested a detailed study of SOXL after reviewing Iwanaga and Sakemoto's
uploaded 44-page working paper. This protocol fixes the first transfer test
before retrieving the full SOXL price history or computing any strategy result.
It does not assume high beta creates high win probability.

The primary question is whether a negative split-normalized overnight gap predicts
a positive **tradable, delayed** opening-session return after explicit cost
scenarios. The primary arm is long only at 09:32 to 10:00 New York time, at 25%
portfolio notional and 5 adverse basis points per side. The full sign-rule and
same-open idealization are diagnostic comparisons, not deployable strategies.

The 2016–2025 interval includes major up and down markets. EARLY/MIDDLE/LATE are
fixed chronological robustness partitions, not prospective unseen validation.
2026 SOXL prices are deliberately not fetched. No moving-average, AVWAP, stop,
profit target, gap threshold or confirmation rule is optimized in this study.

See [protocol.json](protocol.json). All original research and production source
files remain unchanged. Historical bar results cannot establish live execution.
The work does not place any order or authorize strategy deployment.
